### Images
- Current watermarked or stock prototype images must be replaced with official, licensed, or approved final images.
- Product images should be replaced with real Haaratimata product photography.
- Category images should be approved by the business.
- Hero and gallery images should be replaced or licensed before public production use.

### Product Data
- Product names must be reviewed by the business.
- Prices are prototype/sample data and must be validated.
- Bulk/trade prices must be confirmed.
- Sizes, weight, colors, MOQ, and lead time must be verified.
- Availability status must be reviewed.
- Quote-only products must be confirmed.

### Fair Trade / Certification
- Current official website says Haaratimata is Fair Trade Certified.
- Before final launch, the company should provide approved certificate images, logos, certificate numbers, issuing organization details, and verification links if available.
- Do not display fake or unapproved Fair Trade/WFTO logos.
- Sustainability claims should be verified by the company.
